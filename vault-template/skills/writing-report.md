Write in the user's language, for someone who reads only the first paragraph:

1. `## Summary` — 3–6 sentences that answer the question directly.
2. `## Key findings` — bullets, most important first, each with its citation.
3. Sections by theme (not by source); resolve contradictions explicitly ("A says X, B says Y because…").
4. `## Open questions` — what could not be verified or needs a decision.
5. `## Sources` — one de-duplicated, numbered list.

Prefer short sentences, concrete numbers and dates. No filler, no marketing tone.
