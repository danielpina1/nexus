# IKEA Portugal

Furniture and home.

## Search and products
- Always stay under https://www.ikea.com/pt/pt/. If a page shows another country, go back.
- Products have an article number (e.g. 104.567.89) — record it in the episode note; it is the
  reliable id for re-ordering.
- Check stock per store ("Disponível em Alfragide/Loures/Matosinhos/Braga/Loulé…") and online
  delivery availability.
- Furniture comes in several packages; delivery and assembly are paid extras — ask.

## Cart and checkout
- "Adicionar ao carrinho" → cart → "Continuar para o checkout" → delivery or Click & Collect →
  saved payment → final confirmation (SPEND approval). Guest checkout exists, but prefer the
  logged-in IKEA Family account when available (member prices).
